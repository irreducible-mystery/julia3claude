# Black Holes under Paraconsistent Annotated Evidential Logic Eτ (PAL2v)

Black holes are the sharpest known locus of inter-theoretic contradiction in physics: general relativity and quantum mechanics — each supported by overwhelming evidence in its own domain — issue directly opposed verdicts at the horizon and the singularity. We encoded four core black hole puzzles as PAL2v evidence annotations (μ favorable, λ unfavorable) and analyzed them on the lattice, where dc = μ−λ measures net certainty and dct = μ+λ−1 measures contradiction (┬ inconsistent, ┴ paracomplete, t/f true/false).

## A. The Information Paradox — contradiction is *localized in time*

Proposition: "black hole evaporation is unitary; information escapes."

Time-resolving the evidence along the evaporation using Page's theorem produces the key result: **before the Page time (t ≈ 0.54 t_evap) there is no paradox at all** — Hawking's thermal entropy and the unitary Page curve are numerically identical, and the state sits solidly in region *t* (dc = +0.95, dct ≈ 0). The contradiction degree only becomes significant (dct > 0.25) at **t ≈ 0.70 t_evap**, climbing to dct = +0.80 (region ┬) at the endpoint, where unitarity (μ = 0.95, QM tested to extreme precision) collides with Hawking's semiclassical calculation (λ = 0.85).

PAL2v thus reframes the paradox: it is not "QM vs GR everywhere" but a *late-time, localized* contradiction — exactly where the 2019 replica-wormhole "islands" resolution (Penington; Almheiri–Engelhardt–Marolf–Maxfield) operates. Encoding that resolution as evidence revision (thermal counter-evidence λ: 0.85 → 0.25 because the Page curve is now *derived within* semiclassical gravity) migrates the state from ┬ to **t** (dc = +0.70). **Paradox resolution has a precise geometric meaning on the lattice: a trajectory from the contradiction vertex back into the truth region.**

## B. The Firewall (AMPS) Paradox — a quantified trilemma

The 2012 Almheiri–Marolf–Polchinski–Sully argument: three postulates, each individually near-certain — P1 unitarity (0.95), P2 no-drama/equivalence principle (0.90), P3 semiclassical EFT at the horizon (0.85) — are *jointly* inconsistent via monogamy of entanglement.

Fusing all three for H = "the horizon is smooth" yields **dc = +0.09, dct = +0.71, region ┬**: near-total contradiction despite every ingredient being nearly certain. This is the formal signature of a genuine theoretical paradox (as opposed to a mere error): high-quality evidence on *both* annotations.

The trilemma structure becomes quantitative when we drop each postulate in turn — all three escapes restore |dct| ≈ 0, but they land in *different* regions: dropping P1 (Hawking's information loss) or P3 (complementarity, ER=EPR) restores a smooth horizon (*t*); dropping P2 restores a *firewall* (*f*). PAL2v shows the paradox is exactly a forced three-way choice and measures the evidential price of each exit. Classical logic can only say "the conjunction is false"; the lattice says *which way* each resolution moves and how much certainty it costs.

## C. The Singularity — paracompleteness, not inconsistency ★

This is the deepest result of the analysis. Sweeping "GR's description is valid at radius r" from the horizon (r_s ≈ 2.95 km, solar mass) down to the Planck length: at the horizon GR is fully supported (*t*, dc = +0.95 — horizon curvature for astrophysical black holes is *tiny*). Approaching the Planck scale, empirical support μ collapses — but crucially there is **no refuting evidence either**: nothing has ever contradicted GR at r → 0; there is simply *no evidence at all*. So λ stays low, and the state slides **down** the lattice to dct = −0.74: the **paracomplete vertex ┴**, not the inconsistent vertex ┬.

**PAL2v's verdict: the singularity is not a contradiction in nature — it is a region where our theory goes silent.** This distinction is invisible to classical logic (which conflates "proves both" and "proves neither" into a single failure) but is exactly what the two-dimensional annotation captures. It also gives a clean rereading of the Penrose singularity theorem: GR *proves its own incompleteness* — a Gödel-like self-certification of the boundary of its domain, marked ┴, awaiting quantum gravity.

The contrast with puzzle A is the punchline: **the same black hole hosts two opposite epistemic pathologies** — the information paradox trends toward ┬ (over-determined: too many proofs) while the singularity trends toward ┴ (under-determined: theory silence). The master figure shows the two trajectories literally diverging to opposite poles of the lattice.

## D. Black Hole Complementarity — observer-indexed annotations

"Is Alice's infallen qubit inside the horizon?" Alice (infalling) says yes — she crosses without drama (region *t*, dc = +0.90). Bob (external) says no — he sees the qubit scrambled on the stretched horizon and re-emitted in radiation (region *f*, dc = −0.90). **Each observer's annotation set is perfectly consistent** (dct = 0 for both).

Contradiction appears only for the "superobserver" who fuses both frames: (μ, λ) = (0.95, 0.95) → dct = +0.90, region ┬. But no physical agent can *be* that superobserver — verifying both stories requires crossing the horizon *and* collecting the radiation, which causal structure and Hayden–Preskill bounds jointly forbid. PAL2v gives 't Hooft–Susskind complementarity a precise logical form: **annotations are observer-indexed; the contradiction is real in the index-collapsing fusion, but that fusion is operationally impossible.** Physics itself quarantines the ┬ state. (This is structurally identical to how the geometry protects chronology — nature appears to censor its own contradictions.)

## Conclusions: what paraconsistency offers physics

1. **A diagnostic taxonomy.** The lattice separates the black hole's pathologies: information paradox and firewall are ┬-type (*over-determination* — competing valid derivations), the singularity is ┴-type (*under-determination* — evidence starvation). These demand opposite remedies: reconcile the derivations vs. build a new theory. Classical logic cannot even state the difference.
2. **Non-explosion keeps physics usable.** Astrophysicists correctly compute accretion disks, mergers, and gravitational waves with GR *while* the information paradox stands unresolved. That everyday practice is logically illicit under classical logic (one contradiction ⊨ everything) but exactly licensed by paraconsistency: the ┬ state at the evaporation endpoint never leaked into LIGO's waveform predictions. PAL2v is a formalization of how theoretical physics *actually manages* its own inconsistencies.
3. **Resolution = lattice trajectory.** The islands calculation (2019) shows what solving a paradox looks like in this framework: not deleting a contradiction by fiat, but revising evidence until the state migrates ┬ → t. The formalism naturally tracks the *history* of a paradox.
4. **Complementarity formalized.** Observer-indexed annotations turn a vague slogan ("no contradiction because no one sees both") into a theorem-shaped statement: per-index consistency + operational impossibility of index fusion.
5. **A caveat, honestly stated.** PAL2v operates at the *epistemic* level — it manages evidence about theories; it does not compute quantum gravity. Its value is meta-theoretical: quantifying where the contradiction lives (after the Page time, at the postulate triple, *not* at the horizon itself), which is precisely the kind of localization that guided the actual physics breakthroughs.
