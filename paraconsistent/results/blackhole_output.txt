A. BLACK HOLE INFORMATION PARADOX (time-resolved along evaporation)
==============================================================================
H_info: 'information escapes; evaporation is unitary'
Page's theorem: for t < t_Page (~54% of lifetime) the ENTANGLEMENT ENTROPY of
radiation predicted by unitarity is NUMERICALLY IDENTICAL to Hawking's thermal
entropy -- the two theories agree early on. They diverge only after t_Page:
Hawking's curve keeps rising (information loss), Page's curve turns down
(information return). We encode instantaneous evidence at each time:
  mu(t)  = unitary account's support = 1 - (normalized gap it must explain)
  lam(t) = thermal account's counter-evidence = normalized entropy gap
plus constant background weights: QM unitarity is tested to extreme precision
(w_qm=0.95), semiclassical gravity is tested only indirectly (w_sc=0.85).

  early time  (t=0.10 t_evap): dc=+0.950 dct=-0.050 region=t
  Page time   (t=0.54 t_evap): dc=+0.950 dct=-0.050 region=t
  end stage   (t=1.00 t_evap): dc=+0.100 dct=+0.800 region=┬
  contradiction becomes significant (dct>0.25) at t = 0.70 t_evap  -- i.e. AFTER the Page time: PAL2v localizes the paradox in late-time  radiation, exactly where the replica-wormhole 'islands' fix operates.
  A. info paradox @ end-stage            (mu=0.95,lam=0.85) dc=+0.100 dct=+0.800 region=┬      UNDECIDED
    -> Late-time: high mu AND high lam -> quasi-inconsistent band. The paradox is not 'QM vs GR everywhere' but a localized late-time contradiction.

  Post-2019 update (Penington; Almheiri et al.): replica-wormhole islands

B. AMPS FIREWALL PARADOX (monogamy of entanglement)
==============================================================================
Three postulates, each individually near-certain:
  P1 unitarity (Page curve)         : late radiation R maximally entangled with early radiation E
  P2 no-drama (equivalence princ.)  : infalling mode B maximally entangled with interior partner A
  P3 semiclassical EFT at horizon   : B is the SAME mode as R's purifier
Monogamy of entanglement: B cannot be maximally entangled with BOTH E and A.
Encode H_smooth: 'the horizon is smooth (no firewall)'.
  mu  <- P2 evidence (equivalence principle: tested to 1e-15, but only in
         low-curvature regimes; horizon extrapolation discounts it)
  lam <- P1+P3 jointly force B entangled with E, contradicting smoothness
  B. smooth horizon (all 3 postulates)   (mu=0.90,lam=0.81) dc=+0.093 dct=+0.708 region=┬      UNDECIDED
    -> Region ┬-adjacent: near-maximal contradiction. The postulate TRIPLE is jointly inconsistent even though each member is individually near-certain -- the signature of a genuine theoretical paradox, now quantified.

  Testing the three candidate resolutions (drop/discount one postulate):
  B. drop P1 (Hawking: info loss)        (mu=0.90,lam=0.09) dc=+0.815 dct=-0.015 region=t      ACCEPT
  B. drop P2 (AMPS: firewall exists)     (mu=0.10,lam=0.81) dc=-0.708 dct=-0.093 region=f      REJECT
  B. drop P3 (complementarity/ER=EPR)    (mu=0.90,lam=0.10) dc=+0.805 dct=-0.005 region=t      ACCEPT
    -> Each drop restores |dct| ~ 0 and a decisive verdict, but lands in a
    DIFFERENT region: drop-P1 & drop-P3 restore 't' (smooth horizon), drop-P2
    restores 'f' (firewall). PAL2v shows the paradox is exactly a 3-way
    forced choice, and measures how much evidence each escape route burns.

C. THE SINGULARITY: paracompleteness, not inconsistency
==============================================================================
H_GR(r): 'GR's spacetime description is valid at radius r' (solar-mass BH).
  mu(r)  <- empirical support, eroded as Kretschmann curvature K(r) ~ 48G^2M^2/r^6
            approaches the Planck curvature (quantum gravity regime)
  lam(r) <- positive counter-evidence AGAINST the description. Crucially, near
            r=0 there is no experiment refuting GR -- there is NO EVIDENCE AT
            ALL. lam grows only mildly (theoretical unease), it does not -> 1.
The question PAL2v can pose and classical logic cannot:
  is the singularity ┬ (contradiction: over-determined) or ┴ (paracomplete:
  UNDER-determined, theory silence)?
  C. GR validity @ horizon r_s           (mu=1.00,lam=0.05) dc=+0.950 dct=+0.050 region=t      ACCEPT
  C. GR validity @ deep interior         (mu=0.99,lam=0.12) dc=+0.861 dct=+0.110 region=t      ACCEPT
  C. GR validity @ quantum-gravity onset (mu=0.50,lam=0.17) dc=+0.332 dct=-0.328 region=Qt→┴   ACCEPT
  C. GR validity @ r -> l_p              (mu=0.06,lam=0.20) dc=-0.143 dct=-0.743 region=┴      UNDECIDED
    -> As r -> l_p the state slides DOWN the lattice toward ┴ (paracomplete):
    dct -> -0.8, not +1. VERDICT: the singularity is not a contradiction in
    nature; it is a region where our theory GOES SILENT (evidence-starved).
    Penrose's theorem is thus reread: GR proves its own incompleteness --
    a Godel-like self-certification, marked ┴, awaiting quantum gravity.
    Contrast: the INFORMATION paradox trends toward ┬ (over-determined).
    Same black hole, two opposite epistemic pathologies -- only a
    two-dimensional logic can distinguish them.

D. COMPLEMENTARITY: observer-indexed annotations & the superobserver
==============================================================================
H_cross: 'Alice's infalling qubit is inside the horizon at time t_B'.
  Alice (infalling): crosses uneventfully (no-drama). Her evidence: qubit inside.
  Bob (external): sees infalling matter red-shifted, 'scrambled' on the stretched
  horizon, and re-emitted in radiation. His evidence: qubit OUTSIDE (never crossed).
  D. Alice's frame: qubit inside         (mu=0.95,lam=0.05) dc=+0.900 dct=+0.000 region=t      ACCEPT
  D. Bob's frame: qubit inside           (mu=0.05,lam=0.95) dc=-0.900 dct=+0.000 region=f      REJECT
  Each observer's annotation set is fully consistent (regions t and f).

  The forbidden SUPEROBSERVER fuses both frames (max of each evidence):
  D. superobserver fusion                (mu=0.95,lam=0.95) dc=+0.000 dct=+0.900 region=┬      UNDECIDED
    -> Fusion -> ┬-adjacent, near-maximal dct. But no physical agent can perform this fusion: verifying both stories requires crossing the horizon AND collecting the radiation, which the geometry itself forbids (Hayden-Preskill bounds). PAL2v gives complementarity a precise logical form: annotations are OBSERVER-INDEXED; contradiction appears only in an index-collapsing fusion that spacetime renders operationally impossible. The paradox is real in the formalism but quarantined by physics.
